% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Summary
\section{\textbf{Professional Summary}}
\vspace{1mm}
\small{
Research scientist (Ph.D. Computer Science -- Robotics; M.Sc. Robot Systems) with 5 years of research experience designing and developing
\textbf{real-time digital twin applications} of the built environment, preceded by \textbf{2 years in industry as an Automation Engineer}
(\textbf{PLC and HMI programming} for \textbf{industrial control systems}). Co-developer of \textbf{S-Graphs}, an open-source framework that turns
live LiDAR, camera and IMU data into a \textbf{hierarchical semantic knowledge graph} of buildings, and of methods integrating
\textbf{BIM/IFC models and architectural plans} with sensor data to keep the virtual model aligned with the as-built reality.
Experienced in combining \textbf{first-principle, probabilistic/statistical, data-driven (GNN) and computer vision models}, in
\textbf{uncertainty quantification for risk-aware decision support}, and in shipping reproducible software (C++, Python, Git, Docker).
10 peer-reviewed publications (8 IEEE RA-L, 2 IROS), 2nd place in the Hilti x Trimble 2026 Localization Challenge, and a track record
of analytical problem solving, conference presentations and cross-functional collaboration.
}
